Eight concepts, each `N-name.svg` (512 px, rounded dark tile `#111617`, accent `#c1f0ad`, secondary `#669c83` = the colours of the Tau Omega SVG icon; Tau Web's pale green is `#cced9b`, nearly the same). `sheet.png` shows all eight.

1 film-strip tau, 2 timeline tau with an encode-out arrow, 3 compression funnel, 4 dot-matrix tau, 5 viewfinder brackets, 6 clapperboard, 7 stacked frames, 8 progress ring.

The chosen concept goes to `../tau-encoder-logo.svg`; the amber logo there is the earlier proposal.
